# Day 55 – Reflection

## What I Learned

Before today's lesson, I thought email security was mostly about blocking spam. I now understand that email is one of the primary ways attackers gain access to organizations.

I learned the differences between **spam, phishing, spear phishing, whaling, and Business Email Compromise (BEC)**. Although they all use email, each attack has a different objective and targets different victims.

One concept that stood out to me was **Business Email Compromise (BEC)**. Unlike traditional phishing attacks, BEC often contains no malware or malicious attachments. Instead, attackers rely on trust and social engineering to deceive victims into transferring money or sharing sensitive information.

I also learned how **SPF, DKIM, and DMARC** work together to verify email authenticity and reduce email spoofing.

---

## Key Takeaway

Email security is more than filtering unwanted messages. It combines user awareness, authentication technologies, and security controls to protect organizations from phishing, spoofing, malware, and financial fraud.

---

## Personal Reflection

Today's lesson helped me appreciate how important email analysis is in a SOC environment.

A single malicious email can lead to credential theft, ransomware, or financial loss. As a future SOC analyst, I need to be able to identify suspicious emails, understand email authentication results, and investigate email-based threats before they impact the organization.
