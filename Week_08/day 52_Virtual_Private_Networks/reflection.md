# Day 52 – Reflection

## What I Learned

Before today, I knew that VPNs were mainly used for privacy and accessing the internet securely.

Today's lesson helped me understand that in enterprise environments, VPNs play a much bigger role. They allow employees to securely access company resources from anywhere while protecting sensitive data through encryption.

I also learned that there are different types of VPNs, such as Remote Access VPNs and Site-to-Site VPNs, each designed for different business needs.

One concept that stood out to me was **Full Tunnel vs Split Tunnel VPNs**. I now understand the security and performance trade-offs between the two.

Finally, I discovered that VPN logs are an important source of evidence for SOC analysts. They can reveal suspicious login attempts, impossible travel events, and unauthorized remote access, making them critical during security investigations.

---

## Key Takeaway

A VPN is more than just a privacy tool—it's an essential security technology that enables secure remote access while providing valuable logs that help SOC analysts detect and investigate security incidents.

## Personal Reflection

Every new topic reminds me how connected cybersecurity really is.

Networking, authentication, firewalls, SIEM, and now VPNs all work together to protect an organization's infrastructure.

Understanding these connections is helping me think less like a student memorizing concepts and more like a SOC analyst investigating real-world security events.
