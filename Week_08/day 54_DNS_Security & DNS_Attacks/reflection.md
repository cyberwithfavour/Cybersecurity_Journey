# Day 54 – Reflection

## What I Learned

Before today's lesson, I knew DNS simply translated website names into IP addresses. I didn't realize how heavily attackers rely on DNS to hide malicious activity.

I learned that DNS is involved in almost every network connection, making it one of the most important protocols for both attackers and defenders.

One concept that stood out to me was **DNS Tunneling**. I found it interesting that attackers can use normal DNS traffic to secretly communicate with compromised devices or steal sensitive information while avoiding detection.

I also learned that DNS logs provide valuable evidence during investigations by showing which domains users and devices communicate with.

---

## Key Takeaway

DNS is much more than a service that translates domain names—it is a critical source of intelligence for SOC analysts. Monitoring DNS traffic can reveal malware infections, phishing attempts, command-and-control communication, and data exfiltration.

---

## Personal Reflection

Today's lesson changed how I view DNS.

Instead of seeing it as just another networking service, I now recognize it as one of the most valuable tools for threat detection and investigation. Understanding DNS analysis will help me become more effective at identifying suspicious network behavior as a future SOC analyst.
