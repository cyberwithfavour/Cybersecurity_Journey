## Hands-on Investigation

### Scenario

A SIEM generates the following alert:

> **"A new local administrator account was created at 2:17 AM outside normal business hours."**

---

### Investigation Objectives

As a Tier 1 SOC Analyst:

1. Validate the alert.
2. Identify the Windows Event IDs you would investigate.
3. Determine what evidence you need.
4. Assess the severity of the alert.
5. Decide whether to escalate the case.
6. Recommend immediate actions.
