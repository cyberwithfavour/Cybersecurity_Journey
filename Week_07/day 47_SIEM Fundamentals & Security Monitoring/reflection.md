# Reflection – Day 47

Today's lesson introduced me to one of the most important tools used in a Security Operations Center (SOC): the Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) system.

Before today, I knew that SOC analysts investigated alerts, but I didn't fully understand where those alerts came from. Learning how logs are collected, normalized, correlated, and turned into alerts helped me connect the entire investigation process.

I also learned that logs are one of the most valuable sources of information during an investigation. A single log may not reveal much, but when multiple logs are correlated, they can uncover suspicious activities that might otherwise go unnoticed.

Another takeaway was understanding the difference between SIEM and SOAR. While SIEM focuses on collecting, analyzing, and detecting threats, SOAR helps automate responses to those threats.

This lesson gave me a clearer picture of how security monitoring works behind the scenes and why SIEM platforms like Microsoft Sentinel and Splunk are essential tools for SOC analysts.

As I continue this journey, I want to become more comfortable reading logs, understanding alerts, and using SIEM platforms to investigate security events with confidence.
