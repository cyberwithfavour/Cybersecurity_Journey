## Hands-on Investigation

### Scenario

A SIEM generated the following alert:

> **"PowerShell executed a Base64-encoded command on a company workstation."**

---

### Investigation Objectives

As a Tier 1 SOC Analyst:

1. Validate the alert.
2. Identify the information you need before making a decision.
3. Determine the severity level.
4. Decide whether the alert should be escalated.
5. Recommend immediate actions.
6. Document your findings.
