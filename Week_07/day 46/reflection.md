# Reflection – Day 46

Today marked my introduction to Security Operations Centers (SOC), and I gained a better understanding of how cybersecurity teams monitor and respond to security threats.

One of the biggest lessons I learned is that security alerts should never be treated as incidents without proper investigation. A SOC analyst must validate alerts, gather evidence, analyze the findings, and document every step before making decisions.

Learning about the responsibilities of Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 analysts also helped me understand the career progression within a SOC team.

This lesson strengthened my interest in becoming a SOC Analyst and showed me the importance of attention to detail, critical thinking, and proper documentation in cybersecurity operations.
