# Day 13 Reflection

Today I learned that while the OSI Model helps us understand networking concepts, 
the TCP/IP Model is what is actually used in real-world networking.

I also understood how the four TCP/IP layers map to the seven OSI layers. Comparing both models 
made networking much easier to understand.

This lesson helped me see how data moves across the Internet and why understanding
the layers is important for troubleshooting and cybersecurity.
