# Day 27 Reflection

Today I learned that cybersecurity is not just about responding to attacks but designing systems 
that are secure from the beginning.

The biggest lesson was understanding Defense in Depth. I now see why organizations use multiple 
layers of protection instead of relying on a single security control.

I also learned that principles like Secure by Design, Least Functionality, and High Availability 
help build stronger and more resilient systems.
