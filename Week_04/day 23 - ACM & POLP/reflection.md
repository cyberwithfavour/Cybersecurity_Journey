# Day 23 Reflection

Today I learned that authentication alone is not enough. After verifying a user's identity,
organizations must carefully control what that user can access.

The biggest lesson was understanding the Principle of Least Privilege and the differences 
between DAC, MAC, RBAC, and ABAC.

I also realized why Role-Based Access Control is widely used in companies because it makes 
permission management simpler and more secure.
