# Day 20 Reflection

Today I learned that cybersecurity isn't just about preventing attacks—it also involves 
monitoring systems and troubleshooting problems.

I now understand how tools like ping, traceroute, nslookup, ip, netstat, and ss help 
diagnose network issues. I also learned why packet captures and log analysis are essential 
during security investigations.

This lesson showed me that being a cybersecurity professional requires both security 
knowledge and strong troubleshooting skills.
