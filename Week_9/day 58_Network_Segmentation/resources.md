### Wireshark

Used to:

- Capture packets
- Analyze network traffic
- Observe communication between devices
- Troubleshoot network issues

---

### Nmap

Used to:

- Discover hosts
- Identify open ports
- Enumerate network services
- Verify segmentation effectiveness
